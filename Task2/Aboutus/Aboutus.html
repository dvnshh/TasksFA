About Us

CineFinder was created for movie lovers who are tired of searching everywhere for the nearest theatre. We collect information about cinemas across India and make it easy for you to find one close to your home.

Whether you want a big multiplex experience or a classic single-screen theatre, we have you covered. Simply enter your city and find all available options.
